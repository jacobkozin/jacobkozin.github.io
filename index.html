FAQ

Questions people ask.

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

People search Google and Maps before they visit. A site with your hours, prices and a call button shows up there and answers their questions in five seconds. Facebook pages often don't.

What if I need changes later?

The care plan covers small edits every month. Bigger changes get their own quote, same as the first project.

Do you take Robux?

I work in USD through PayPal invoices. That keeps pricing clear and protects both of us.

Will my Roblox code be readable?

Yes. Everything is organized into modules with comments and a short setup guide, so you or another scripter can keep building on it.
