Privacy

Version 1.0 · Last updated November 18, 2026

This site belongs to Jacob Kozin, an individual web developer in Scottsdale, Arizona. Here's what happens to information when you visit or contact me.

What this site does and doesn't do

  • This site has no analytics, no advertising trackers, no cookies set by me, and no account sign-up.
  • There are no forms that send data to a server I run. The "Call", "Text me" and "Email" buttons open your own phone or email app, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.
  • The "Discord" button opens an invite to my Discord server in a new tab. If you join, Discord handles your account and messages under its own privacy policy.

Services that handle requests when you visit

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and operations. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If you contact me

If you call, text or email me, I receive whatever you send, such as your name, number, email and message. I use it only to reply, quote and do the work you ask for. I don't sell it or share it for marketing. Messages are stored by my phone and email providers (Google). If you hire me and pay through PayPal, PayPal processes the payment under its own privacy statement. I keep invoices and payment records as long as tax rules require.

Your choices

Ask me to stop contacting you and I will. Ask me to delete our messages and I'll delete what I control, except records I have to keep for taxes or an active job. Contact: jacobkozin.support@gmail.com · (602) 935-9847.

Demo pages

The demo sites on the homepage are samples. Their quote forms only open your phone's text app or your email app, and they don't store anything. Concept previews made for real businesses are labeled as not affiliated and are hidden from search engines.

Changes

If this site starts doing something new, like adding analytics, I'll update this page first.
